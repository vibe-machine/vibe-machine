Vibe Machine Early copy, for friends

A Mac app for building software with AI agents.

You install one app and open it. Agents do most of the work. You steer them with small decisions: which piece of work comes next, which answer is right, which result to keep.

Download for Mac

Apple silicon Mac · macOS 26 or later · about 350 MB

What you do with it

  1. Ask a question. In Lingo Studio you ask what you want your data to answer. It builds a model of your data (an ontology) that answers the question, and the app checks the answer.
  2. Build on it. Plan the work, design it, and supervise the agents that write the code.
  3. Show it. Flex records a demo video of what you built.

Install

  1. Download the disk image, VibeMachine.dmg, and open it.
  2. Drag the dial into the ring on the machine. This copies the app to your Applications folder.
  3. In Applications, open One. That is the Vibe Machine; the file’s name has not caught up yet.

What stays on your Mac

The app and your data stay on your own Mac. What leaves it: requests to your AI provider, packages you publish to GitHub, and videos you upload to YouTube.
