You have a team of agents now. Your ambition and creativity have been unleashed.

You already know agents need context to plan, build and ship. Here is what comes next: everything else needs it too. The workflows that run your app. The co-pilot inside it. The agent answering your users, the one keeping the lights on, and the one acting as your chief of staff. The analytics that tell you what to build next. Even your cost accounting.

Context sounds mysterious. It isn’t. Take cost accounting: it’s a database. Context is the data you already have, handed to an agent as background.

So what if there were a simple way to model all of it: your stuff, your process, your information? What if it all lived in one database? What if that database knew how everything was connected? What if it could learn?

That is an operational ontology.
That’s The Vibe Machine.

Open VibeMachine.dmg, drag the dial into the ring, then open One in Applications. That is the Vibe Machine; the file’s name has not caught up yet.
